Our Key Initiatives

Empowering educators and scholars through specialized programs, research symposiums, and national academic collaborations.

📚 Academic & Research Programs

🎓

Doctoral Research Conclaves

Annual research conclaves providing doctoral candidates with direct feedback from recognized Ph.D. supervisors across commerce, finance, and marketing.

💡

Faculty Development Programs

National workshops on advanced data analysis tools (SPSS, R, Python, PLS-SEM), case study pedagogy, and emerging fintech applications.

📖

Publication Guidance

Mentorship on writing high-impact research papers for Scopus, Web of Science, and UGC-CARE indexed international journals.

🌐

Faculty Directory & Networking

Centralized digital repository connecting 340+ scholars across 25+ states to foster inter-institutional research and co-authorship.

🏛️

Curriculum Modernization

Advisory frameworks for aligning business and commerce curriculum with NEP 2020, industry requirements, and digital transformation.

🤝

Industry-Academia Bridge

Interactive seminars connecting corporate leaders and educators to enrich teaching case studies and student research internships.
