Why Become an ICMT Member?

More Than Membership. A Community for Academic Growth.

ICMT membership is designed to provide academics and researchers with opportunities to connect, collaborate, learn, contribute and grow.

As an ICMT member, you become part of an academic community committed to strengthening commerce and management education, research and professional practice.

Connect: Build meaningful professional relationships with academicians, researchers, doctoral scholars and academic leaders.

Collaborate: Discover opportunities for research collaboration, knowledge exchange, academic partnerships and interdisciplinary engagement.

Learn: Participate in conferences, webinars, workshops, FDPs, seminars and other academic development initiatives.

Research: Engage with research-oriented activities, methodological discussions, scholarly resources and collaborative research opportunities.

Contribute: Share your knowledge, expertise, research insights, teaching practices and academic experiences with the wider community.

Grow: Develop your academic profile through participation, leadership, recognition and professional engagement.

Who is ICMT Membership for?

ICMT welcomes individuals and institutions engaged in or supporting Commerce, Management, Business, Accounting, Economics and allied disciplines.

Faculty Members: Professors, Associate Professors, Assistant Professors, Lecturers and academic professionals.

Researchers: Researchers and scholars engaged in commerce, management, business and allied disciplines.

Doctoral Scholars: Ph.D. scholars and research scholars seeking academic networking, research engagement and professional development.

Academic Leaders: Principals, Deans, Directors, Heads of Departments and institutional leaders.

Industry & Professional Community: Professionals, consultants, entrepreneurs and practitioners interested in academic–industry engagement.

Institutions: Universities, colleges, business schools, departments, research centres and other academic institutions.
